# Task

Create an interactive Sales Dashboard in Power BI using the provided Sales dataset.

The dashboard should provide a clear overview of Sales, Profit, Orders, Customers, Products, and Regional Performance.

Dashboard Requirements

**1. KPI Cards**

Create KPI cards for:

Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Quantity
Total Orders
Average Order Value
Profit Margin %
2. Sales Analysis

**Create visuals to show:**

Sales by Year
Sales by Month
Sales by Category
Sales by Sub-Category
Sales by Brand
3. Profit Analysis

**Create visuals to show:**

Profit by Category
Profit by Sub-Category
Profit by Region
Profit by Year
4. Customer Analysis

**Create visuals to show:**

Sales by Customer Segment
Sales by Gender
Sales by Loyalty Status
Customers by Age Group
5. Product Analysis

**Create visuals to show:**

Top 10 Products by Sales
Top 10 Products by Profit
Quantity Sold by Category
6. Regional Analysis

**Create visuals to show:**

Sales by Region
Sales by State
Sales by City
Profit by Zone
7. Order Analysis

**Create visuals to show:**

Sales by Order Priority
Average Delivery Days by Region
Sales by Financial Year
8. Additional Analysis

**Create visuals to analyze:**

Discount vs Profit
Shipping Cost vs Sales
DAX Requirements

**Create appropriate DAX measures for the dashboard, such as:**

Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Quantity
Total Orders
Average Order Value
Profit Margin %
Average Delivery Days

Use appropriate DAX functions wherever required.

**Slicers**
Add suitable slicers to make the dashboard interactive.


**Dashboard Design**

Give the dashboard a proper title: Sales Dashboard
Use suitable visualizations for each analysis.
Keep the dashboard clean and easy to understand.
Use proper chart titles.
Maintain consistent formatting.
Ensure all slicers work correctly.
Avoid unnecessary visuals.
